05 / Use it well

Keep the core open.

01 / SPACE

Give it room.

Leave at least one module width around the visible mark. That is 12 units in the 96-unit master canvas.

02 / SCALE

Small with purpose.

Standard mark from 24px. Pixel-grid micro mark at 16px. Horizontal lockup from 140px. Keep its proportions intact.

03 / COLOR

One clear gesture.

Use one solid color. Keep all eight modules together. Avoid gradients, outlines, shadows, and low-contrast combinations.
