# Hakopod voice and messaging

## Positioning

Hakopod is a self-hosted application platform for people who want to deploy and run applications on infrastructure they own.

## Main line

Your apps. Your rules.

## Supporting line

A home for your apps, on infrastructure you own.

## One sentence

Deploy, observe, and run applications on your own infrastructure with Hakopod.

## Campaign lines

- Build freely. Run independently.
- Run it on your terms.
- Make yourself at home.
- Your infrastructure. In your hands.

## Useful product labels

- Deploy an application
- Review changes
- Open service
- View logs
- Add a node
- Manage your team

## Voice

Calm, capable, and direct. Write like a helpful engineer. Explain a choice when someone needs to make it. Use sentence case. Prefer a concrete verb over a broad promise.

Say what a control does. Describe measured results precisely. Do not invent performance numbers, availability guarantees, customer counts, or security certifications for marketing.
